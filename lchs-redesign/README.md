# Licking County Humane Society — Independent Website Redesign Concept

An **unofficial, static, responsive website prototype** designed to demonstrate how an animal-first LCHS homepage might look and feel. Not commissioned, approved, or endorsed by LCHS.

## View or publish

1. Download and unzip `lchs-redesign.zip`.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser to preview locally (internet required for externally hosted photos and fonts).
3. Create a GitHub repository, e.g. `lchs-website-concept`.
4. Upload **index.html**, **styles.css**, **script.js**, and **README.md** to the repository root (not inside another folder).
5. Open **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`, and save.
6. GitHub will provide a public URL, usually `https://USERNAME.github.io/lchs-website-concept/`.

## Included interactions

- Responsive navigation and mobile menu
- Switch between illustrative dog and cat cards
- Clickable animal detail dialogs
- Real outbound links for adoptions, giving, volunteering, contact, maps, and social accounts
- Accessibility basics: alt text, focus styles, keyboard-accessible controls, reduced-motion preference

## Important accuracy notes

- **Sample animal cards are not live adoption listings.** Names are inspired by publicly published listings, but all photos are stock images and all descriptions are illustrative, not verified temperaments, bios, or adoption eligibility.
- The site intentionally uses neutral copy rather than inventing rescue stories, prices, or donation outcomes.
- The four impact statistics are LCHS's publicly posted **2025** totals, not live totals.
- Visit hours and official information were checked in October 2026, but may change.
- Outbound adoption/donation actions redirect to the *official* LCHS website, so this demo never processes applications or payments.
- Photos and fonts are loaded over the internet (Unsplash / Google Fonts); for a production site, replace them with permission-cleared LCHS photography and self-host approved assets. Verify stock-image license requirements before public/commercial use.
- The icon mark in the header is an original concept motif, not LCHS's official logo. For a real deployment use approved brand assets.

## Where to customize

- `index.html` — copy, services, impact statistics, site structure, links.
- `styles.css` — design, layout, colors, responsiveness.
- `script.js` — sample pet data, card switching, dialogs, mobile navigation.

## Before a real launch

Get LCHS approval, replace stock photography with approved animal photos, connect pet cards to the shelter's actual animal management system or available-pets feed, verify all service links, add privacy/analytics policies, and perform accessibility, SEO and usability testing.
