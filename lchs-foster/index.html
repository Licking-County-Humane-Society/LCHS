03Meet the teamAttend an in-person orientation, usually about 30 minutes.
04Find a matchDiscuss an animal and placement that fit your household.

Before you begin

  • Applicants must be at least 18.
  • If you rent, check your landlord’s animal restrictions.
  • Have information about the people and pets in your home handy.
  • The current application asks for vet records for household cats and dogs. Staff will explain how to provide them.

Different ways to help

From kittens and puppies to adult animals, seniors, and animals recovering from surgery, foster needs vary.

Interested in a short dog stay? Select Buddy Brigade / short break and discuss timing with the team.

Placement length must be confirmed with staff
Can I foster if I work weekdays?

The coordinator’s email offers weekday orientation options and says weekends can be arranged. Ask the team about a time that works for you.

What should I know about my own pets?

The coordinator recommends rabies and DHPP for dogs, and rabies and FVRCP for cats. Ask staff to confirm vaccination and record requirements for your household.
