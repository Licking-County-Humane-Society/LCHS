# LCHS — Website Redesign Concept, Version 2

An **independent, unofficial** responsive prototype for Licking County Humane Society (LCHS). Not commissioned, approved, or endorsed by LCHS. All donations, adoption information, signups and inquiries link to the official website.

## Preview
Open `index.html` in a browser. The site uses `styles.css` and `script.js` in the same folder. Internet access is required to load Google Fonts, Unsplash stock photographs, and outbound official links.

## What's new in V2
- Emotion-led hero and featured-pet storytelling concept.
- Action-first navigation and four quick actions.
- Interactive pet-owner help routing (food support, vet care, rehoming, humane investigations).
- Volunteer interest chooser with role descriptions, constraints, and official links.
- Expanded More Than a Shelter programs, plus Sophie’s Thrift.
- Editorial concepts for Reels, adoptions, and behind-the-scenes stories.
- Wishlist/urgent-needs CTA pointing to the official source instead of hard-coded lists.
- Newsletter CTA, current 2025 impact figures, and a dated/dismissible detour notice.

## Accuracy safeguards
- All pet profile names, photos and personality descriptions are ILLUSTRATIVE and not verified active LCHS listings.
- Stock photos must be replaced by organization-approved photos before any real implementation. Stock-image license and allowed use should be reviewed.
- The featured animal and story cards are **concepts only**. They do not describe real animal availability.
- The current detour notice is programmed to expire automatically on November 14, 2026. Staff should recheck the actual construction timeline and access route before publication.
- No personal data is collected. There is no adoption application, donation processing, email signup form, analytics, or live pet API in this demo.
- Third-party links, policies, operating hours and program eligibility must be reviewed with LCHS before production.

## Deployment
Upload `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `script.js` to the root of a static host or GitHub Pages repository. Configure hosting to serve `index.html` as the entry point.

## Next-phase ideas if LCHS approves
Actual pet-system integration (with permission); a CMS for featured animals, stories and events; approved photography and Reels; accessible content/interaction testing; performance optimization and locally hosted images; SEO and structured data; a consent-aware analytics plan; editorial ownership and maintenance procedures.

## Version 2.1: Micro-donation campaign concept
- Replaces the generic donation banner with a playful “They can’t buy their own treats. But you can.” campaign.
- $3/$5/$10 selector provides a visual suggestion; it does **not** transmit an amount or process payments.
- All donation actions link to the organization’s public donation page.
- Venmo, Cash App and Zelle are deliberately presented as **unconnected ideas**, not payment links. Add verified charity-owned accounts or QR codes only after explicit LCHS approval.
- Claims that a specific donation amount buys a particular service are intentionally avoided.
